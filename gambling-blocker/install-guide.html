Gambling blocker setup

Block gambling on your phone and computer

Pick your device below and follow the steps in order. Tick each one as you finish it. It takes about 10 minutes per device, and you need to do it on every device you own.

What you will do on each device

  1. Install BetBlocker, a free blocker run by a registered charity. It blocks gambling websites and apps.
  2. Lock it, so it can't be removed on a bad day. For this you need one person you trust to type a passcode you don't know.
  3. Test it by opening a betting site and checking that it won't load.

Ask your trusted person before you start. They only need to be there for the lock step.
